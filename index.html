From fallen to fighting.

03 / NO HEAD LEFT BEHIND

The void doesn’t
get the last word.

↩

Back to the island. Or back to you.

An uncollected head falling into the void returns to safe ground in its dimension. Once collected, fire, lava, or void recovery returns it to its last carrier. Offline or full inventory? The return waits.

∞

Built to survive the wait.

Revival heads resist damage and don’t naturally despawn. They stay as items so their identity and protection are preserved.

▧

No hiding them in Ender Chests.

Heads can’t enter Ender Chests—even inside bundles or shulker boxes. Each new death still needs a new head and new ingredients.

GOOD TO KNOW

Before you jump in.

Does the totem revive automatically?

No. Keep it until you’re ready, then hold it and right-click. It disappears only when revival succeeds.

Does revival restore lost items?

No. Players return with full health and hunger, plus ten seconds of resistance and fire resistance. Their dropped inventory and lost experience are not restored.

Can I test it before using a server?

Yes. Use a fresh Hardcore test world opened to LAN with a second player. Both players need this mod and Fabric API. A complete teammate revival needs two players.

Where do I find the recipe in JEI?

With JEI for Fabric 26.3 installed, search for Revival Totem and view its recipe. The generic Steve head is labeled “Player Head.” Craft with your teammate’s actual dropped head.

What if there’s no safe island location?

Recovery tries the last safe footing, then nearby loaded land. Older End heads can fall back to the central island. In unusual custom dimensions with no land, the head stays floating above the void.
